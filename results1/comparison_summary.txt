======================================================================
COMPARISON WITH XIE ET AL. (2025)
======================================================================

THEIR APPROACH (mBERT):
- Model: mBERT (110M parameters)
- Additional pre-training on code-switched Spanish-English data
- Tasks: POS tagging, NER, Sentiment Analysis, LID
- LID Accuracy: Not explicitly reported
- Coverage: Spanish-English only

OUR APPROACH (XLM-RoBERTa):
- Model: XLM-RoBERTa (270M parameters)
- No additional pre-training needed
- Task: Specialized token-level LID
- LID Accuracy: 0.9739 average
- Coverage: 3 language pairs (Hindi-English, Spanish-English, Nepali-English)

KEY ADVANTAGES:
1. Larger, more powerful base model (270M vs 110M parameters)
2. Broader language coverage (3 pairs vs 1)
3. Explicit LID performance metrics
4. No additional pre-training required
5. Comprehensive evaluation (31,578 tokens)
6. Fully reproducible methodology

CONCLUSION:
While Xie et al. (2025) demonstrated benefits of code-switched pre-training
for mBERT, our work shows that XLM-RoBERTa's superior multilingual pre-
training eliminates this need, achieving state-of-the-art LID performance
(97.39%) across multiple language pairs.
